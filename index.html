The record.

Results from the 25th Knesset, built with partners from the coalition and the opposition.

Parliamentary diplomacy

I led the delegation's work with more than 30 national delegations: meetings, coalition building, speeches and drafting, with the Foreign Ministry. During the 2026 war with Iran, I briefed members of parliament from 28 countries.

Lawmaking across the aisle

I co-initiated about 20 laws that passed, working with members of the coalition and the opposition. I co-chaired caucuses with members of Yesh Atid and National Unity, among them the Trusted Tech Caucus with Purdue's Krach Institute.

The region and the Jewish world

I chaired, and later co-chaired, the Knesset's Abraham Accords Caucus, working with the ambassadors of the UAE, Bahrain and Morocco. In 2023 I co-founded a Knesset caucus for Diaspora Jews.

My story

I grew up in Montreal, in a Moroccan Jewish family, studied law at McGill and moved to Israel alone at 23. Before the Knesset I worked on treaty negotiations at the Foreign Ministry, advised the coalition chair on legislation, joined JGive as its first employee, served on Jerusalem's city council and led ZOA's work in Israel.
