Short bio, about 50 words

Dan Illouz served in Israel's 25th Knesset from 2023 to 2026 and headed Israel's delegation to the Inter-Parliamentary Union. Born and raised in Montreal in a Moroccan Jewish family, he moved to Israel alone at 23. He speaks and writes in English, Hebrew and French, and has appeared on CNN, Fox News and the BBC.

Longer bio, about 200 words

Dan Illouz served in Israel's 25th Knesset from 2023 to 2026. As head of Israel's cross-party delegation to the Inter-Parliamentary Union, he worked with more than 30 national delegations. Emergency items aimed at Israel fell 58 and 67 votes short at the 149th and 150th assemblies (2024 and 2025), against 12 at the 148th. During the 2026 war with Iran he briefed members of parliament from 28 countries. In the Knesset he co-initiated about 20 laws that passed with coalition and opposition partners, among them the law barring Israeli state contact with UNRWA. He chaired and later co-chaired the Abraham Accords Caucus, and co-chaired the Trusted Tech Caucus with Purdue University's Krach Institute. He fought his own coalition's draft-exemption legislation and lost his committee seats for it.

Before the Knesset he worked on treaty negotiations at Israel's Foreign Ministry, advised the coalition chair on legislation, joined the giving platform JGive as its first employee, served on Jerusalem's city council and directed ZOA's work in Israel. Born and raised in Montreal in a Moroccan Jewish family, he holds law degrees from McGill and an MA in public policy from the Hebrew University. He speaks English, Hebrew and French.
