Privacy notice.

This site belongs to Dan Illouz, who is responsible for the information collected here. It is written to meet Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13.

What I collect

Only what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email and message, and any optional details you add, such as your organization, phone number, dates or budget.

Why

To reply to you and keep a record of our correspondence. I don't use these details for anything else, and I don't add you to any mailing list.

Who else sees it

Only the services that deliver, store and help me organize my correspondence, such as my email provider, the form service and AI tools I use to keep track of enquiries. I don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

Do you have to give these details?

No. But without your name, email and a message I can't reply to you.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses no advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If visits are counted, it is with privacy-friendly analytics that do not identify you. Videos from other sites load only when you choose to play them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Send the request through the contact form.

How long I keep it

Up to two years after our last contact, or longer only if we work together. Then it is deleted.
