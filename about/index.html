From Montreal to Jerusalem to the Knesset.

I served in Israel's 25th Knesset from 2023 to 2026 and headed Israel's delegation to the Inter-Parliamentary Union. Before that I worked in government, at a fast-growing nonprofit platform and in Jerusalem city politics.

My story.

I grew up in Montreal, in a Moroccan Jewish family. I studied law at McGill, and in 2009, at 23, I moved to Israel on my own. I served in the IDF, where I am a reserve captain in the Military Advocate General's international law department, and earned a master's in public policy at the Hebrew University.

My first jobs were in government: treaty negotiations in the Foreign Ministry's legal department, then legislation as an adviser to the coalition chair. I joined JGive, an online giving platform, as its first employee. I served on Jerusalem's city council, representing the city's new immigrants, and then ran ZOA's work in Israel on my own, reporting to the United States.

In the Knesset I learned that the work that lasts is done with people who don't have to agree with you. I passed laws with members of the coalition and the opposition, and led a cross-party delegation in rooms that walked out on Israel. I also learned that principles cost something. When I broke with my coalition over subsidies tied to the draft exemption, I lost my committee seats. A Jerusalem Post editorial praised "the bravery to break party lines and stand alone."

The record.

Highlights from the 25th Knesset, 2023 to 2026.

Parliamentary diplomacy

  • Head of Israel's delegation to the Inter-Parliamentary Union, 2024 to 2026, as President of the Knesset's IPU Group.
  • Led the delegation's work with more than 30 national delegations: meetings, coalition building, speeches and drafting, with the Foreign Ministry.
  • Emergency items aimed at Israel fell 58 votes short at the 149th Assembly (Geneva, 2024) and 67 at the 150th (Tashkent, 2025), against 12 at the 148th.
  • During the 2026 war with Iran, briefed members of parliament from 28 countries.
  • Elected to the IPU Working Group on Science and Technology, 2025.
  • Head of the Knesset delegation to the OECD Global Parliamentary Network.

Lawmaking across the aisle

  • Co-initiated about 20 laws that passed, working with members of the coalition and the opposition.
  • Co-initiated the law barring Israeli state contact with UNRWA, with Yulia Malinovsky (Israel Beytenu) and Ron Katz (Yesh Atid). It passed 87 to 9, 2024.
  • Co-sponsored, with Ze'ev Elkin, the amendment to Basic Law: Jerusalem that bars new foreign consulates in Jerusalem. It passed 29 to 7, 2024.
  • Member of the Economic Affairs Committee, including its subcommittees on hi-tech, regulatory reduction and banking competition.
  • Alternate member of the Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee.
  • Co-tabled the platform workers bill (2024) and co-sponsored the ride-sharing bill (2026).

Technology

  • Co-chair, with Ron Katz (Yesh Atid), of the Trusted Tech Caucus, launched in 2025 with Purdue University's Krach Institute for Tech Diplomacy.
  • Co-chair of the Knesset Crypto Caucus.

The region and the Jewish world

  • Chair, later co-chair with Michael Biton (National Unity), of the Abraham Accords Caucus. Worked with the ambassadors of the UAE, Bahrain and Morocco.
  • Represented the Knesset in Abu Dhabi, Manama and Marrakech, 2023.
  • Co-founded a Knesset caucus for Diaspora Jews, 2023, and hosted visiting delegations at the Knesset.
  • Spoke in the Knesset, in English, against antisemitism on the American right, 2026.

Iran

  • Hosted an online meeting of coalition and opposition members of the Knesset with Iran's exiled opposition, 2025.
  • Wrote to the Iranian people with Michael Biton (National Unity) in Newsweek, and with Iranian-American activist Mariam Memarsadeghi in The Times of Israel, 2025.
