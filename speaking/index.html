Formats.

I have spoken across North America for years: community tours, Birthright groups, and campuses including Columbia and Penn. Every talk ends with time for questions, and the hard ones are welcome.

  • Keynote

    45 to 60 minutes, with Q&A. Galas, conferences, federation and community events.

  • Community talk or weekend

    One talk, or three to four sessions. Synagogues, JCCs and community weekends.

  • Campus conversation

    Talk and open Q&A. Hillels and student groups, including on campuses where Israel is a hard subject.

  • Moderated conversation or panel

    Any length. On stage with a host, or with other speakers.

  • Briefing on Zoom

    30 to 60 minutes. Boards, donors, members and national calls.

  • Briefing in Israel

    45 to 90 minutes. Missions and delegations visiting Israel.

Questions organizers ask.

What does a talk cost?

Fees depend on the format and travel. I work with community, campus and conference budgets, so tell me yours in the form.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

I live in Jerusalem and travel for talks in North America and Europe. Booking alongside other dates on a tour shares the travel costs.

What if a flight is cancelled?

If war or a cancelled flight keeps me from travelling, the talk goes ahead on Zoom.

Which languages?

English, French and Hebrew.

What do you need on the day?

A lapel microphone, a handheld one for questions, and HDMI if there are slides. For Zoom, a test call 15 minutes before.

Can we record the talk?

Usually yes, for your members. Please ask when you book.
