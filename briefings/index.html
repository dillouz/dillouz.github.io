Practical details.

Where does the briefing take place?

At your hotel or venue in Jerusalem or the center of the country, or on Zoom before you travel.

How long is it?

Usually 45 to 90 minutes, with plenty of time for questions.

Which languages?

English, Hebrew or French.

Can you arrange meetings with ministers or members of the Knesset?

No. The briefing is with me.

We are a tour operator. How do we book?

Send the form with your dates, group size and hotel. You will hear back within one business day.
