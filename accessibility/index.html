Accessibility statement.

I want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard or other assistive technology.

What I have done

  • The site is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, which go beyond Israeli Standard 5568.
  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a link to skip to the content.
  • Text and background colours meet the contrast requirements.
  • Images have text descriptions, and the page structure uses proper headings and landmarks.
  • Movement on the site is minimal and stops if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The site is in English. Hebrew and French versions are coming, with Hebrew shown right to left.

What may not be fully accessible

Some videos and articles on other sites that this site links to may not have captions or may not meet the same standard. If you need any content in another format, ask and I will provide it.

Ask for help or report a problem

Use the contact form and describe the problem. I will reply within one business day and fix what I can as soon as possible.
